# Surface brief — homepage of finckfisch (sites/finck-fisch)

Scope: the studio's homepage (and the shared shell it imposes on /services/*). Mode: Persuade.
Audience/job: an artisan on an iPhone, between two jobs, deciding whether to call. Action: call
(tel:) or write (e-mail / form). Proof: the three-proposal mechanism shown on real demo renders, the
0-48-72 h timeline, the written price, the twelve trade demonstrations (stated once as demos).
Constraints: nothing invented; prices and 72 h provisional (say it once); studio stays anonymous;
French typography; perf budgets (html <= 140 KB, js-initial <= 56 KB); iPhone Safari first.
Unresolved: real phone/address/SIRET; final prices.

## Direction contract

THESIS: L'Éphéméride de l'atelier. The page is the tear-off day block every French workshop hangs
next to the postman's calendar: the studio's time is counted sheet by sheet (72 h = three sheets).
Refuses the category default: dark luxury agency hero, cards row, pricing cards, testimonials.

OWN-WORLD: red cardboard backing (#c8322a family) owns large regions as ground; thin white
perforated sheets (near-white, never cream) carry the content; black ink; one postal blue for the
twelve-month calendar. One type family with extreme width contrast: ultra-condensed black numerals
(the éphéméride date) against normal-width text; the brand serif lives only in the logo. Depth only by
overlapping sheet edges: no shadow, no gradient, no glow. Perforations and stubs carry captions.
State reads by inversion (ink on paper -> paper on ink). Solid red is reserved for the call action and
the 72 h figure.

STORY: in one glance: a site, 72 hours after your material, from 1 200 € paid once, and a button to
call. Then the visitor tears three sheets and sees one bakery printed three ways (the mechanism), reads
the three dated sheets 0/48/72 h, the day's sayings (what the studio refuses), flips the twelve-month
calendar of trades, reads the tariff table on its back, and detaches the reply card to call or write.

FIRST VIEWPORT: red cardboard strip on top (wordmark, Réalisations, Offre, Contact). Centered on red,
one white day-sheet bound at its top edge, two sheet edges peeking below it. On the sheet: small
éphéméride header line, a giant "72" (the largest thing on screen), "heures" under it, the sentence
"pour mettre votre site en ligne, à partir de vos textes et de vos photos", a perforated rule, the stub
"1 200 €, payé une fois · le site est à vous", and the pull-tab: a solid red "Appeler" button plus
"Écrire". On iPhone the sheet fills the width and the button sits above the fold; a sticky bottom bar
Appeler / Écrire appears after the hero.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: tearing a sheet. On load, yesterday's sheet tears off upward once revealing
today's "72". In "Trois feuilles, un seul contenu", tap/swipe/drag tears the top sheet to reveal the
same bakery in the next direction (1/3, 2/3, 3/3, then restack); keyboard and buttons work; all three
images are present for assistive tech. Reduced motion: no tearing, sheets shown side by side.

FORM: grounded candidate 5 of 7 (éphéméride / calendrier des Postes), seed key 2570cfe2. Raises: red
reserved for the decisive beat (manga); depth by overlap only (Łowicz papercut); every graduation
means something (oscilloscope); state by inversion (one-bit desktop); one loud entry per section
(Ray Gun); margins carry information (16 mm film).
