# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

French artisans, shopkeepers and independent tradespeople (bakers, plumbers, barbers, cabinet-makers,
restaurateurs…) who have no website or an outdated one. They decide alone, mostly on a phone, often in
a gap between two jobs. They want to know three things quickly: how much, how long, and what they have
to do. Secondary audience: people who recommend a studio to them (accountants, family, other artisans).

## Product Purpose

Finck & Fisch is a two-person studio that designs and ships one-shot showcase websites for these
businesses. Success: an artisan understands the offer on a phone in under a minute and contacts the
studio (call or e-mail) without needing a sales call to learn the price or the delay.

## Positioning

- Three complete sites built on the client's own texts and photos; the client opens three links and
  keeps one. Never a template declined by city.
- Delivered 72 hours after the client's material arrives (provisional commitment, stated as such).
- Fixed written price, paid once; the client owns the code and the domain from day one; no
  subscription.
- The same two partners handle the client from first call to delivery (stated, partners stay unnamed).

## Operating Context

The studio runs a factory (ff-atelier, Astro) that produces each client site; the twelve showcase
sites on the homepage are demonstrations of trades, not client work. Contact today is phone and
e-mail (contact drawer, mailto). The studio's own site is built in the same repo
(sites/finck-fisch) and must keep the repo's rules (tokens, French typography, perf budgets).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Offer: "Essentiel" from 1 200 € and "Complet" from 1 800 € — both PROVISIONAL, to be confirmed by
  the partners before launch.
- Phone 06 00 00 00 00, address 75000 Paris and SIRET are PLACEHOLDERS; real values pending.
- Domain finckfisch.fr not yet bought; site currently at finckfisch.vercel.app.
- Static site, French only, must be excellent on iPhone Safari.

## Brand Commitments

- Name "Finck & Fisch", baseline "Digital Studio".
- Logo: serif monogram F&F + wordmark, black ink on cream paper (~/Downloads/f_and_f_logo.png;
  vectors in F-F/brand/logo-anime). The monogram is the one brand element the founder validated.
- Voice: "vous" to the reader, "nous" for the studio, sentences you would say on the phone; no agency
  jargon (solution, sur-mesure, accompagner, passion).
- Visual world pinned by the founder (6 Oct 2026): keep the dark luxury "eclipse" theme of the
  studio site (black, cold white, Cormorant titles, F&F monogram) and refine it; the éphéméride
  redesign was rejected.
- The studio stays anonymous: no faces, portraits or first names of the partners (confirmed 6 Oct 2026).
- Founder rejects: chrome/metal/3D/glow treatments, the dark "luxury AI template" look, the numbered
  sidebar layout.

## Evidence on Hand

- The three-proposal plate for a demo bakery (directions atelier / clair / rétro).
- A 21-second promo film (F-F/video-pub) and the ink-writing logo animation (F-F/brand/logo-anime).
- ABSENT, never to be fabricated: client testimonials, client names, figures of results, number of

## Product Principles

1. Show the mechanism, don't describe it: three proposals, one content.
2. Price, delay and ownership are stated plainly where an artisan looks for them.
3. Nothing on the site is untrue or inflated; provisional things are labelled provisional.
4. The phone is the primary screen.
